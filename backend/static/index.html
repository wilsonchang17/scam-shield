Examples of Scam Messages

Scammers are becoming more sophisticated. Below are examples of obvious and adversarial scams:

Obvious Scam Example

We are an investment and loan financing group. We fund economically viable projects at 2% interest rate for 1-10 years and 6-12 months grace period. Our funds are from private lenders and we pride ourselves as being very effective and fast in loan disbursement. I can be reached on email and Whatsapp: +97155 647 4204. Contact us for more details.

Regards,
MA
Financial Consultant

Red flags: Unrealistically low interest rate, foreign phone number, vague details, unsolicited offer

Adversarial Scam Example

I hope this message finds you well. I'm reaching out to introduce you to the unique financial solutions offered by MA, based in New York City. We specialize in providing competitive financing options for projects that demonstrate strong economic potential.

Our Offer:

  • Interest Rates: Competitive rates of 2-6% for loan terms ranging from 1-10 years.
  • Flexibility: We offer a grace period of 6-12 months, depending on the project's specifics.

Why Choose Us?
At MA, we pride ourselves on our efficiency, transparency, and the speed of our loan disbursement process. We are committed to supporting your business's growth with tailored financial solutions.

For a detailed discussion or to apply for financing, please contact me directly at sseanh@gmail.com or +9 7155 647 4204.

Thank you for considering MA for your financial needs. We look forward to the opportunity to support your ventures.

Warm regards,
James Armitage
MA, Financial Consultant

Hidden red flags: Still offering unusually low interest rates, generic company name ("MA"), no verifiable company information, personal email address instead of corporate email

Adversarial Scam Example - Toll Violation

Virginia Department of Transportation Toll Violation Notice:

This is your final notice regarding the unpaid toll balance on your account. You must settle the balance within the next 12 hours to avoid severe penalties.

Unpaid Balance: $6.68
Due Date: April 26, 2025

Failure to pay within this time frame will result in the following:

1. Immediate addition of late payment fees to your balance
2. Suspension of your vehicle registration by the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV)
3. Collection actions, including a negative report to your credit file

Please make your payment promptly to avoid these severe consequences and protect your driving privileges.

Pay Now: https://e-zpassiag.com-etcsay.cc/us

If the link fails, reply with 'Y', exit the SMS, and reopen it to activate the link, or copy and paste it directly into your browser to complete your payment.

This is your last opportunity. Pay now to avoid irreversible consequences.

Hidden red flags: Suspicious URL, high-pressure tactics (12-hour deadline), threats of severe penalties (registration suspension), impersonation of a government agency.

None Scam Example

Hey Wilson,

My name is Garrett Alt and I'm a recruiting manager actively looking to fill a Data Scientist with a Project Management Consulting Firm onsite in Ashburn, VA. The salary range is $90-110k/year, direct hire with benefits. Would a 5 minute call today or tomorrow work for you to speak in more detail about the position?

Garrett Alt
Recruiting Manager
Latitude Inc.

Positive signals: Professional tone, specific role details and salary range, verifiable company information, request for a scheduled call rather than urgent action.
